The garments on the rack are built from these models, both licensed under

| Used for | Model | Author |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tees | [Basic T-Shirt](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/basic-t-shirt-71bdf5940b5d41b8b46628a615e9b0ed) | [MadeByYeshe](https://sketchfab.com/MadeByYeshe) |
| Long sleeves and layered tees | [Party Starter - Layered T-Shirts](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/party-starter-layered-t-shirts-fbfa86ea615e4028b8ad400de902bfd4) | [RealityRoller](https://sketchfab.com/RealityRoller) |

**Changes made:** at runtime the models are re-posed to hang on a hanger
(flattened, widened, sleeves lowered), simulated as cloth and re-textured with
our own fabric colours and prints; only their geometry and normal maps are used.
The layered tee's `scene.gltf` was trimmed to reference just its normal map.
